# Better Radio

> Minecraft's soundtrack, on your terms.

Give every build, mining trip, and adventure the music you want to hear. Better Radio brings Minecraft's background tracks and music discs into a personal music player, with simple controls to skip, pause, and revisit your favorite songs.

## Choose your mood

**Free flow:** Let the music find you as you explore, with a preference for background tracks that suit your biome. In the NeoForge player, choose Frequent, Balanced, Occasional, or Non-stop to set the pace.

**Playlist:** Build a mix of background music and discs, then play it on repeat or turn on shuffle. Choose its pace independently from Free flow, with a short 2–4 second pause by default. Your saved playlist follows you between worlds. Available in the NeoForge player.

## Your music, within reach

Press **F6** on NeoForge to open the player:

- **Player:** Follow the current track's progress, pause or resume, skip tracks, switch listening modes, toggle shuffle, and see what's coming next. Jump to a queued song whenever you want.
- **History:** Find a song you just heard, play it again, add it to the queue, or save it to your playlist.
- **Playlist:** Search the music library, add your favorites, and remove tracks from your mix.
- **Settings:** Choose separate intervals for Free flow and Playlist, adjust how strongly music follows your biome, and choose which mode starts with your world. Your preferences stay saved between sessions.

Background tracks and discs from compatible music mods can appear in your library too. A small notification shows what's playing or when playback is paused.

## Controls · NeoForge

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Open the player | **F6** |
| Previous track | **F7** |
| Play / pause / resume | **F8** |
| Next track | **F9** |

Remap your shortcuts in **Options → Controls → Key Binds → Better Radio**. Adjust playback volume with the **Music** slider in **Music & Sounds**; jukebox volume stays separate.

You can also use the playback shortcuts while the player is open.

## Compatibility

- **Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge:** Full music player. Requires **R-UI Lib 0.4.0 or newer**.
- **Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge:** Playback shortcuts and notifications.

Install Better Radio on your client; your server does not need it.
