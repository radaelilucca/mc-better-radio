# Changelog

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-14

### Added

- The full Better Radio player and settings are now available on Forge 1.20.1.

### Fixed

- Paused music stays paused after closing the player and resumes only after a direct play action.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-14

### Fixed

- Paused music stays paused instead of being replaced by another track, including after reloading the world or game.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-09

### Added

- A personal music player on NeoForge 1.21.1 with playback controls, a queue, listening history, playlists, and shuffle.
- Track names, playback progress, and clear states for paused music, startup, and intervals between songs.
- A searchable music library with background tracks and discs, including music from compatible mods. Save favorites directly from your history.
- Free flow with adjustable biome preference and separate interval settings for Free flow and Playlist, including Non-stop.
- Saved playlists and preferences across sessions, plus a choice of startup mode.
